Why a group

Better together than alone.

  • Better deal flow. We screen ~2,000 companies so you see the top few.
  • Shared diligence. Deal teams split the work.
  • Smaller checks. SPVs let you join with less capital.
  • Expert members. Operators and investors across dozens of sectors.
  • New friends. Tigers from every class year.

How deals work

Our diligence process.

PAA and deal leads never make recommendations. Every decision is yours.

  1. Pitch night

    Companies pitch on Zoom and take questions.

  2. Signal interest

    Fill in a short online form.

  3. Deal team

    A volunteer deal lead coordinates with the company.

  4. 4–6 weeks

    2 management calls plus reference calls.

  5. Decide

    Commitments are pooled and shared with the founders.

Selectivity

FAQ

Investor questions.

Is there a minimum investment?

It depends on each round. Past checks ranged from about $5K to over $100K.

Do I have to invest after a pitch night?

No. You choose deal by deal.

Does PAA tell me what to invest in?

No. We find startups and organize diligence; the decision is yours.

I'm not accredited. Can I still take part?

Yes. Our events and education are open to the whole community.
