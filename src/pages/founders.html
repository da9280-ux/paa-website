What we look for

High potential, early stage.

Any company can apply. The strongest usually have one of these.

  • Disruptive technology
  • A new, fast-growing market
  • A new business model
  • An active raise pre-seed through Series A
  • Diverse leadership 38% of our portfolio is led by women

The process

From application to decision.

  1. Apply

    Share your deck and round details.

  2. Screening

    Volunteers pick companies for the next pitch night.

  3. Pitch night

    Pitch on Zoom, then moderated Q&A.

  4. Diligence

    A deal team runs 2 management calls plus references over 4–6 weeks.

  5. Decisions

    Members decide individually; we share the total with you.

FAQ

Founder questions.

Do I need a Princeton connection?

No. We prefer one, but consider any promising startup.

How much can I raise?

Members invest individually, from about $5K to $100K+ each, so the total depends on interest.

Does PAA recommend deals?

No. We organize diligence; each member decides on their own.

When will I hear back?

Within a few weeks of the application deadline.

Ready to pitch?

We read every application.
