What we look for

High potential, early stage.

Any company can apply. The strongest usually have several of these.

  • A strong, talented team the first thing we look for
  • Disruptive technology
  • A fast-growing market
  • Meaningful competitive differentiation
  • A strong exit path
  • A capital-light business model preferred

The process

From application to decision.

  1. Apply

    Share your deck and round details.

  2. Screening

    Volunteers pick companies for the next pitch night.

  3. Pitch night

    Pitch on Zoom, then moderated Q&A.

  4. Diligence

    The deal team runs diligence over 4–6 weeks.

  5. Decisions

    Members decide individually; we share the total with you.

FAQ

Founder questions.

Do I need a Princeton connection?

No. You DON'T need a Princeton connection. We consider any promising pre-seed or seed startup.

How much can I raise?

Members invest individually, from about $5K to $25K+ each, so the total depends on interest.

Does PAA recommend deals?

No. We organize diligence; each member decides on their own.

When will I hear back?

Within a few weeks of the application deadline.

Ready to pitch?

We read every application. Applications are open year-round, and any we receive now count toward the next pitch cycle.
